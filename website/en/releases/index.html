v0.1.3

2026-10-23

Fixes

  • Avatar pictures no longer stretch beyond their circle or overlap other elements.
  • An expired session shows one notice and signs out once, also after signing out and back in.
  • Messages sent or received while a chat is loading no longer disappear from it.
  • The portable Windows zip no longer updates itself into a second installed copy with an extra desktop shortcut.
