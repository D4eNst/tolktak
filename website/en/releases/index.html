v0.1.1

2026-10-22

Fixes after the first evening with friends

  • Messages from a deleted group no longer appear in a new group. The server does not reuse deleted IDs, and the app clears cached history for removed channels.
  • Speaking indicators use audio measured on the device instead of delayed LiveKit active-speaker events.
  • Maximum noise suppression preserves quiet speech by adjusting the signal level before and after DeepFilterNet processing.
  • The watched stream receives its full quality layer. The extra VP8 encoder was removed; layers and bitrate were adjusted, and encoder statistics were added.
  • In the background, the app reduces animation and stops receiving previews and cameras. A setting controls this behaviour.
  • Sign-in has a password visibility control and a Caps Lock hint. Registration asks for the password twice.
